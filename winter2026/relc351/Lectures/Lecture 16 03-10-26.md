Confucianism: toughest religion so far

1. Debt of existence
	* Ancestral Inheritance 
	* You are not an independent being
	* Physical existence (mother)
	* Development of language (mother tongue)
	* Anything that comes before this loyalty puts you at conflict with yourself
2. Relational Intimacy
	* Hierarchy over Equality
		* Relationship not built on hierarchy will not create 
		* In confucius time there wasa the idea of mandate of Heaven, very close tie between the spiritual leader and political leader
	* Most important relationships
		1. Ruler and subject
		2. Father and Son
		3. Husband and Wife (In traditional confucian household, changes based on context, husband represents household to the world, represents family in public and political life, once resources come into the household, the wife is head of houseld (complete administration of household resources and gives husband back his allowance))
		4. Elder and Younger Brother
		5. Senior and Junior (between friends, almost always based on age)
3. Social-Spiritual Synonym
	* For example, mimic 
	* The social is the laboratory that allows for spiritual growth
	* "Why would you talk about God when you haven't learned how to treat your siblings"
	* Animistic system where everything on earth has a spiritual dimension and if we violate those spirits, there will be karmic consequences
4. Contextual Ethics
	* Ti-Yong / Ethics as a Gerund
		* There is no ethics outside of functioning
		* You don't carry over any of the ssence outside of doing it. There is no morality without "moral-ing." There is no righteous without "righteous-ing"
		* You cannot rest on your laurells, you aren't anything unless you are doing it
		* It is the application that is ethical, not the principle
